SNR3

Snare one-shot. 909-ish default.

Thin synthetic core (Shell, Wires, Stick) plus a user Sample. Mix, per-layer EQ, parallel post FX, length snap, WAV export with Norm.

Part of Stupid Audio — five free instruments in one pack. The other four live over there.

VST3 and standalone.

Sound

Shell is the pitched body. Wires are bandpassed noise. Stick is a short click. Sample is your WAV/AIFF/FLAC, linear resample, no pitch; a missing file is silence. Mix is mute / solo / gain / phase / Delay / Send. Duck dips Shell when Stick hits. Effects is Dist → Delay → Reverb send plus 5-band post EQ. The waveform is the layered mix before post FX.

Standalone

Play is a one-shot. Loop (BPM 40–240, default 140) repeats until you hit Play again. Hosts hide Play / Loop / BPM and use MIDI. MIDI note-on retriggers and sets Shell pitch (A4 = 440 Hz). The Tune knob is for Play, the waveform, and WAV export. Export WAV writes 24-bit stereo including FX tail, 44.1 or 48 kHz. Norm (default on) peaks to −0.1 dBTP.
