# Main

This is the snare: waveform, Style, Shape, Length, Hold, and the four-layer rail. Play and Loop live here in standalone. In a DAW you hide those and hit it with MIDI instead.

The waveform is the layered mix you hear on Play, before post FX. Select a rail layer to overlay that stem, because staring at a blob and guessing is a bad hobby.

- **Style** — Classic, Fat, Snap, Tight, Hard. Scales the stick and the decays. Does not reset your knobs.
- **Shape** — Sine (default) or triangle on the Shell. Dist Shape on the FX page is a different control, and it would like you to remember that.
- **Length** — Free, or trim to 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 of host BPM (Loop BPM in standalone, 120 if the host has no tempo).
- **Hold** — one-shot dies after the decay; Hold keeps the tail going until the next note. Length snaps still cut.

**Tune** is for Standalone Play, the waveform, and WAV export. MIDI still sets Shell pitch from the note without moving the knob. See [midi.md](midi.md).
