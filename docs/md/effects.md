# Effects

The Effects page is a parallel send after the layered mix. **Dry** is the unprocessed mix. **FX** is Distortion → Delay → Reverb, then a five-band post EQ. Mix **Send** (per layer) is how much of that layer goes into the send.

- **Dist** — Type (Soft, Hard, Fold, Diode) and Shape. Shape here is not the Shell Sine / Tri control. They share a noun. They do not share a job.
- **Delay** — time, feedback, the usual excuses for making a snare last longer than physics wanted.
- **Reverb** — a room, a hallway, or an accident.

Post EQ is the same five-band editor as the per-layer EQ: drag a node, wheel for Q, double-click to reset a band.
